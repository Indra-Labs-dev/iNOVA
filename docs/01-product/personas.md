# Personas

**Status:** [PLANNED] — draft personas, not user-researched yet
**Owner:** Archange Elie Yatte
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-08

## Purpose

Give feature and UX decisions a concrete "who is this for" anchor. These are working hypotheses to be validated, not confirmed research.

## Scope

High-level personas only. Not a substitute for real user interviews once iNOVA has users.

## Persona 1 — The builder

Developer or technical hobbyist who wants an AI-assisted environment for coding, security-aware development, and infrastructure understanding. Primary hubs: [Programming Hub](../08-modules/programming-hub.md), [Cybersecurity Hub](../08-modules/cybersecurity-hub.md), [AI Hub](../08-modules/ai-hub.md).

## Persona 2 — The informed operator

Wants a personalized, trustworthy digest of what's happening in AI, security, and technology without doom-scrolling multiple feeds. Primary hubs: [News Intelligence](../08-modules/news-intelligence.md), [Watchlists](../08-modules/watchlists.md), [Research Hub](../08-modules/research-hub.md).

## Persona 3 — The self-organizer

Wants iNOVA to help structure their day, learning, and goals, with the mascot as a light, non-manipulative companion. Primary hubs: [Productivity Hub](../08-modules/productivity-hub.md), [Learning Hub](../08-modules/learning-hub.md), [Gamification](../08-modules/gamification.md).

## Cross-cutting expectation

All three personas expect: fast responses, clear boundaries on what the AI can do unsupervised, and a UI that feels premium without feeling like a "hacker dashboard" cliché (see [product-philosophy.md](../00-overview/product-philosophy.md)).
