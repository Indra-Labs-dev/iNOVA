## Purpose

Give any reader — human or AI — a one-page understanding of what iNOVA is trying to become, before touching architecture or code.

## Scope

Product vision only. For concrete functional scope, see [scope.md](scope.md) and [feature-matrix.md](../01-product/feature-matrix.md).

## iNOVA — Intelligent Digital Universe

iNOVA is an AI-powered digital environment combining artificial intelligence, autonomous/semi-autonomous AI agents, cybersecurity, programming and developer tooling, research and web intelligence, news aggregation and analysis, defensive OSINT / public-source intelligence, learning, productivity, device and infrastructure management, a futuristic 2D/3D interface, an evolving AI mascot, and gamification.

The goal is **not** a collection of unrelated tools. The goal is a coherent **Digital Operating Environment** where all capabilities communicate through a shared core (identity, permissions, events, memory, design system).

> **iNOVA is an intelligent digital universe that learns, researches, creates, protects, automates, and evolves with its user.**

The user should feel like they are interacting with a living digital environment rather than navigating a conventional dashboard.

## Why this project exists

iNOVA exists to replace a fragmented set of separate tools (chat AI, security scanners, news readers, task managers, code editors) with a single environment where these capabilities share context about the user and about each other, so that the whole is more useful than the sum of its parts.

## What it must become

A modular platform — not a single application — where:

- New hubs and agents can be added without rewriting the core.
- The AI layer is never hard-coded to one model provider.
- Every AI action that has real-world effect is permissioned, previewable, and auditable.
- The visual/futuristic layer (3D world, mascot) supports the experience without ever obscuring what the system is actually doing.
